The Problem

Healthcare in Pakistan is Broken

Pakistan's healthcare system relies on fragmented paper records, illegible prescriptions, and zero digital infrastructure connecting stakeholders.

Paper Prescriptions

85% of prescriptions are handwritten, often illegible, leading to medication errors and no digital trail for follow-ups.

No Patient History

Patients carry physical folders of reports. Doctors have no access to prior medical history, leading to redundant tests and misdiagnosis.

Medication Errors

WHO estimates 1.3M deaths/year from medication errors globally. Pakistan lacks any system to verify prescriptions digitally.

Zero Interoperability

Clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies operate in silos. No shared platform exists to unify patient data across providers.

Our Solution

One QR Code. Complete Medical Identity.

Every patient gets a unique QR code. Doctors scan it to access history, write digital prescriptions, and track medications. Everything syncs in real-time.
