Product Walkthrough

Follow the complete user journey through MediScan — from patient signup to AI-powered prescription insights.

1 Patient

Patient Creates Account

A new patient signs up through the mobile app or web portal. They provide basic information — name, phone, CNIC (national ID), date of birth, gender, and blood group.

  • Multi-step registration with validation
  • CNIC auto-formatting (XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X)
  • Custom date picker for mobile
  • Secure password with visibility toggle

2 Patient

Unique QR Code Generated

After signup, each patient gets a unique QR code — their digital medical identity. This QR code can be shown to any doctor on the platform for instant access to their medical profile.

  • Unique QR per patient stored permanently
  • Works on mobile app and web
  • Doctor scans to pull up full history
  • No need to carry paper records

3 Doctor

Doctor Scans Patient QR

The doctor opens the scanner on their device and scans the patient's QR code. Instantly, the patient's full profile appears — demographics, medical history, past prescriptions, and uploaded records.

  • Camera-based QR scanner built-in
  • Instant patient profile lookup
  • View all past prescriptions
  • Access uploaded lab reports and records

4 Doctor

Doctor Writes Digital Prescription

The doctor fills in the prescription digitally — diagnosis, medications with dosage and frequency, vitals (blood pressure, sugar level), and any lab tests requested. A professional PDF is auto-generated.

  • Add multiple medications with dosage/frequency/duration
  • Record vitals: BP, sugar levels (optional)
  • Request lab tests (CBC, Lipid Panel, etc.)
  • Custom medicine names or pick from list
  • Auto-generated PDF with clinic letterhead

5 Patient

Patient Views Prescriptions

The patient immediately sees the new prescription in their dashboard. They can view all medications, download the PDF, and see their vitals recorded. Every prescription is permanently stored and accessible.

  • All prescriptions in one dashboard
  • Download professional PDF
  • View vitals history (BP, sugar)
  • See tests requested by doctor
  • Track medication status (active/completed)

Track Medications & Upload Records

Patients can track their daily medication intake, upload lab reports and medical documents into organized folders, and download professional PDF prescriptions. Everything is permanently stored and accessible from any device.

  • Upload lab reports, X-rays, and documents
  • Organize records into folders
  • Download prescription PDFs
  • Complete medical history in one place

7 Admin

Admin Manages the Platform

Clinic administrators verify doctor credentials against Pakistan Medical Commission records, manage users, monitor platform activity, and review audit logs for compliance.

  • Verify doctor PMC license numbers
  • Approve or reject doctor registrations
  • View platform-wide analytics
  • Complete audit trail of all actions
  • User management and access control
